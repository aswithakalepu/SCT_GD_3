SKILLCRAFT TECHNOLOGY - Business Card Design

INTRODUCTION

This business card was designed for SKILLCRAFT TECHNOLOGY, a Hyderabad-based technology company founded by Arjun Mehta - Founder & Creative Director.

The objective was to create a card that reflects the brand's identity - premium, innovative, and trustworthy. Instead of a standard corporate card, the design focuses on luxury minimalism that leaves a strong first impression and communicates professionalism at a glance.

BUSINESS CARD DESIGN CONCEPT

The concept is based on "Precision Meets Luxury"

- Front Side (Emerald Green): Represents growth, stability, trust and technology. The deep emerald texture gives a rich, premium feel. All personal information is placed here for direct communication.

- Back Side (Pure White): Represents clarity, innovation and simplicity. It is kept minimal with only the brand logo and website, to maintain focus and elegance.

- The Golden Wave Lines: This is the hero element. The continuous thin gold foil lines flowing from one corner to another symbolize:
    - Connectivity and Networking
    - Growth and Forward Movement
    - Craftsmanship and Technology merging together
    - The wave also creates a sense of dynamic energy, showing the company is modern and future-ready.

DESIGN APPROACH

a) Color Palette:
- Deep Emerald Green (#0A3D2E): For trust, premium and growth
- Luxury Gold Foil (#C8A96A): For success, luxury and high-value
- Pure White (#FFFFFF): For clarity and balance

b) Typography:
- Clean Sans-Serif font for modern and tech feel
- Hierarchy maintained: Name in Bold, Designation in regular, Contact details in light weight for easy readability.

c) Logo & Branding:
- The interlocked diamond-shaped logo is kept in gold foil to become a focal point. It represents structure, logic and interconnected systems.

d) Finishing & Print Specs (To mention to printer):
- 400 GSM Matte Textured Paper
- Gold Foil Stamping on wave lines and logo
- Matte lamination on both sides
- Spot UV optional on logo

e) Philosophy: The design follows a minimalist luxury approach. No clutter, only essential information, with a strong visual line that makes the card memorable. The contrast of Green and White makes it versatile and instantly recognizable.
